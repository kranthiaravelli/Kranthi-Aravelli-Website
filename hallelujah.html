Know about "Hallelujah Padedha"

Read and understand what situations made me write what lyrics of the song. Not any lyric is accidental, but was given by the Holy Spirit to strengthen me and as well as the one who truly needed them as some point in their lives.

Author's advice

Hallelujah Padedha — I will sing Hallelujah If you have seen my instagram post regarding the behind the scenes of the song, I have discussed there in bullet points of what the song conveys. So including those points I would like to cover all the details of the song from the starting till the end in this Blog. First of all I really thank you that you have to decided read this blog. I am very much grateful that you stayed reading, because you wanted to know about the testimony behind the song.

Firstly the title itself speaks its purpose, Hallelujah Padedha which means: I will sing Hallelujah. I will sing hallelujah to the one whom my eyes have seen, is what the main theme of the title. Later on the song goes into a prayer track. It was started with the praise and ended with the request or petition, by taking a smooth transition in the lyrics with beautiful melody that the Holy Spirit had inspired me to make. Always remember, the song that is inspired by the Holy Spirit will carry a lot of message to speak on, whether based on the scripture completely or based on the person's personal life, related to the scripture. Not only just the message but we get the preaching of the lord from the song's lyrics itself.

In my Instagram post regarding the song's message I've divided the song into three segments on their purpose. If you have not seen the post Click Here to see it. So I have named the segments as:

  • True Testimony
  • Confessional Prayer
  • Petition to Heaven

Let us get into the each segment and understand what was the message that the Holy Spirit was trying to give us. True Testimony True Testimony — The Honest talk in the song. Yes the first lines of the song were exactly what the thing which had happened in my life. That was something only my eyes have seen and I am hopeless that I can make you see the same thing, but I can give you the picture of how you can imagine that. He said he is light and he is right! — Yes he is exactly 100% right when he said that he is the light. Light of our lives, light of the world, light of our spirit. He is Light!

Try to picture this, I'm telling what I saw: Imagine you were sleeping on the floor with a mat under you in a dark room with just a very low lit bulb which gives you very low intensity light. You have slept for about more than two hours during the day. So you are unable to get into the sleep immediately. You are trying a lot to sleep but your efforts were futile. Now after just rolling in your place where you sleep, your efforts were not giving you the results so now, you don't have any other option but to wait until you actually get into the sleep. So now your mind starts to think some random stuff and started to picture everything wiered happened during the day. I still believe that I slept for a reason during the day and the struggle for the sleep which kept me awake at that moment was not accidental and was very perfectly planned for a reason. Psa. 40:3 — "He gave me reason to sing a new song". This is the reason. So my mind started to think of all the wiered things, by that I started to feel overwhelmed. I was opening my eyes looking here and there and closing my eyes again by getting afraid of what I've just seen, and repeated the same. That went on and on.

But suddenly, I was reminded that I've learnt some psalm from the bible that day. Which is the 91 Psalm. The Royal Psalm which was written by Moses himself. I learnt the Psalm in Telugu. Those were the days when I newly came to know that who Jesus is, I wonder that did God himself told me to meditate? because I also learnt the Psalm 1, Which says in the beginning verses — "His delight is in the law of the lord, He meditates on his law day and night". Basing this verse, I was taught or made understood that, Yeah! I should meditate on the word. For sure I can say you that the one who taught me this was the spirit of the Lord, because I was taught the word in that particular moment and he made his work done in that same instance. Remember this thing also, Anything which you got revealed by the word or made understood by the word, then that is definitely by the spirit of the lord. Because He is only the one who makes his word clear, He is the only one who teaches the scripture, because He himself had inspired people to write them down.
