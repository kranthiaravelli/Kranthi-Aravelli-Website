What we do

Cover Songs

We recreate the old and the timeless Christian melodies which carry deep spiritual substance, with the help of Generative Ai. Ai involvement in our covering the songs is soon going to be terminated.

Original Composition

We publish the songs that were truly written, tuned and composed by Kranthi Aravelli. These tracks are truly original and do not involve any Generative Ai in the writing of the lyrics and making of the tune.

Video Editing

We also edit videos for the songs and publish them through our label, theS Records Media. Though this do not have any physical form, our media services are still available at our making.

Blog Posts

We publish Blog posts that will strengthen today's youth. Not that these are the best ones, these will serve their position in strengthening the faith as the support to your walk with Christ.

Chords

God do not just make us write and tune the spiritual songs, He also gave us the grace to provide you the resources regarding them, for you to sing them in your congregation. We provide Chord sheets to our original compositions.
